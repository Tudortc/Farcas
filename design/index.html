bool · design study

Three ways to build a beautiful IRC client.

bool is an open-source, self-hosted IRCCloud — always-on, fast, IRCv3-native. Design is priority #1. These are three genuinely distinct visual directions for the core three-pane chat view, each compact by default with a real density toggle. Open any of them in your browser; toggle theme and density live in the top bar.

Visual directions
DIRECTION A

Terminal-Modern

Dense, monospace-accented, hacker-native. Near-black with phosphor-green and amber. IRC-authentic gutters, prompt-style composer, maximum information density.
